### Required win rate: arithmetic, not an estimated edge

For gross mean winner G, gross mean loser L (positive magnitude), and mean round-trip cost c in percentage points of deployed capital, break-even p = (L+c)/(G+L).

| Assumed gross win | Assumed gross loss | Break-even before costs |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 4.5% | 5% | 52.63% |
| 4.5% | 10% | 68.97% |
| 5% | 5% | 50.00% |
| 5% | 10% | 66.67% |

Costs raise these thresholds. A model omitting losses beyond 10% understates L unless the tail is measured to be absent, which it is not. Three exact 5% falls compound to −14.2625%, not a hard maximum loss. Two wins out of three is a description of these differently sized historical trades, not a calibrated future win probability.
