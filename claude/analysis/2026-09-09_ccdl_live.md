## What this book is actually saying

**The queue is growing faster than it is clearing.** Between 09:51 and 13:50, about 1.33 crore shares traded — but the buy queue *grew* by 61 lakh and the level at 1.31 grew by 2.29 crore. More people joined the line than were served.

**Joining that queue now means standing behind ~9 crore shares.** Exchange matching is strict price-time priority. At the observed clearing rate of roughly 1.3 crore shares per four hours, and with the queue lengthening rather than shortening, a new order placed at 13:50 does not get filled today. It does not get filled tomorrow either, if the lock holds.

**It gets filled on the day the lock breaks.** That is the only circumstance under which 9 crore shares of demand get served: someone arrives with enough supply to fill them. That someone is not a retail seller.

**The offer side collapsed from 21 lakh to 4 lakh.** The small amount of genuine supply that existed in the morning has been absorbed. What remains is a book with essentially no sellers and an enormous, growing line of buyers — the maximum-tension state, and the state immediately preceding either another lock or a break.

## The tick problem

At ₹1.32, one tick (₹0.01) is **0.76%**.

The 5% band on a ₹1.26 previous close spans 1.20 to 1.32 — **twelve ticks, total.** The entire day's permitted price range is twelve price increments wide. Slippage of two ticks is 1.5%, or 30% of the band.

At this price level the strategy is not fighting the market, it is fighting the price grid.

---

## Order-book shape: manufactured or organic?

Unlike CHANDRIMA on 27 Aug (40 lakh shares in **4** orders — clearly manufactured), CCDL's queue is 6.56 crore shares across **182** orders at the top level. Average ~36 lakh shares per order.

At ₹1.31, a 36-lakh-share order is ₹4.7 lakh. That is a large retail or small HNI order, not a ₹5,000 punt. So: partly organic, but organic *at size*, and heavily concentrated.

The reading: the crowd has arrived. When retail is 182 orders deep on the bid and there are 3 orders on the offer, the position is already crowded. Crowded is late.

## Verdict

**Do not queue for this.** Not on a view about the company — on arithmetic:

1. Fill probability today ≈ 0 (behind 9 crore shares).
2. Fill probability on the break day ≈ 1 — and the break day is the bad day.
3. One tick is 0.76%; the whole band is twelve ticks.
4. It fails the ₹10 price floor by a factor of eight.

If CCDL is worth tracking, track it as a **specimen**, not a position. It is a live example of the exact book shape that precedes both outcomes, and watching it resolve — without money in it — is worth more than the trade.
